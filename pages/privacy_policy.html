Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2024

1. Information We Collect

At Elevate Realty, we collect information to provide you with exceptional real estate services. The types of information we collect include:

Personal Information

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Property preferences and search criteria
  • Financial information for pre-qualification purposes
  • Communication preferences

Usage Information

  • Website browsing behavior and preferences
  • Search queries and property views
  • Device information and IP address
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide personalized property recommendations
  • Facilitate property viewings and transactions
  • Send market updates and property alerts
  • Improve our services and website functionality
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

3. Information Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • Licensed real estate agents and brokers
  • Third-party service providers (with your consent)
  • Legal authorities when required by law
  • Business partners for joint marketing efforts

We never sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • SSL encryption for data transmission
  • Secure servers and databases
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Limited access to personal information

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and review your personal information
  • Request corrections to inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Request data portability
